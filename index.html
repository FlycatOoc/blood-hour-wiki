THE PHENOMENON · 01

A World
Beyond Ordinary Time

Blood Hour is a supernatural phenomenon that first appeared in 2016. By 2026, it has existed for approximately ten years, remaining unknown to most people living their ordinary lives.

Every day at exactly 19:00:00, ordinary time stops. The sky turns blood red, ordinary humans transform into identical coffins, and the environment is suspended in place. Only Awakened individuals and Blood Hour entities remain active.

Those who experience it receive twelve additional hours. When the phenomenon ends, ordinary time resumes at exactly 19:00:01. Only one second has passed outside Blood Hour.

FIXED EXCHANGE RATE

Blood Coin Economy

RM100 = 1 BC

Blood Coin (BC) is the official currency within the Blood Hour economy. It exists as a supernatural digital balance associated with an Awakened individual's identity.

Malaysian Ringgit can be converted into BC, but BC cannot be converted back into Malaysian Ringgit. BC has no direct purchasing value in ordinary life.

Balances persist between Blood Hour sessions. Transactions, purchases, sales, crafting expenses and material ownership must remain consistent.

The Coffin System

Ordinary humans transform into identical coffins when Blood Hour begins. Each coffin carries an identity plate with the person's full name, age, date of birth, biological sex and nationality.

Awakened

Awakened individuals remain active during Blood Hour. They can use abilities, enter the Seven Towers, fight monsters, obtain materials and participate in the Blood Hour economy.

Permanent Death

An Awakened who dies during Blood Hour remains dead. Destroying an ordinary person's coffin also causes permanent death. Resurrection is impossible through the established system.

Ability Progression

Stages 1–4 use an Experience [Current/100] tracker. Reaching 100/100 automatically activates the next stage and resets EXP to 0/100.

True Awakening

Requires the Apex stage, 300 Ability Uses and one Orb of Awakening. The Orb must be manually activated and is consumed immediately.

Personal Weapons

Established personal weapons materialise during Blood Hour and dematerialise when it ends. They cannot be carried into ordinary time.
