THE PHENOMENON · 01

A World
Beyond Ordinary Time

Blood Hour is a supernatural phenomenon that exists alongside ordinary reality. It first appeared in 2016. By 2026, it has continued for approximately ten years, remaining unknown to most people living their normal lives.

Every day at exactly 19:00:00, ordinary time stops. The sky turns blood red, ordinary humans become identical coffins, and the environment is suspended in place. Only Awakened individuals and entities associated with Blood Hour remain active.

Those who experience it receive twelve additional hours to move, work, investigate, explore, trade, fight or simply live. When the phenomenon ends, ordinary time resumes at 19:00:01. Only one second has passed in the world outside.

The Rules of Blood Hour

Blood Hour follows a set of consistent supernatural rules. These conditions affect the environment, ordinary humans, Awakened individuals and the consequences of death.

POWER DEVELOPMENT · 03

The Five Ability Stages

An Awakened ability develops through five recognised stages. Progression is measured through experience and genuine ability use, with a separate requirement for the final stage.

IMPORTANT

Ability progression stages are not ability ranks. Abilities do not use an F–EX ranking system. Monster ranks are a separate classification and do not determine an individual's ability stage.

FIXED EXCHANGE RATE

Blood Coin Economy

RM100 = 1 BC

Blood Coin is the official currency used within the Blood Hour economy. It exists as a supernatural digital balance associated with an Awakened individual's Blood Hour identity.

Malaysian Ringgit can be converted into Blood Coin, but Blood Coin cannot be converted back into Malaysian Ringgit. BC has no direct purchasing value in ordinary life once Blood Hour ends.

Balances persist between Blood Hour sessions. Transactions, purchases, sales, crafting expenses and material ownership remain subject to the established economy.

The Coffin System

Ordinary humans transform into identical coffins when Blood Hour begins. Each coffin carries an identity plate recording the person's full name, age, date of birth, biological sex and nationality.

Awakened

Awakened individuals can move and act during Blood Hour. Their abilities may support combat, defence, healing, mobility, awareness, investigation or other forms of survival.

Permanent Consequences

Death during Blood Hour is permanent. Destroying an ordinary person's coffin causes that person's death. The established system provides no resurrection.
