Welcome to Blood Hour.

Every day, at exactly 19:00, the world stops. The sky turns blood red. Humanity disappears into coffins. And for twelve hours, another world comes alive.

This website is a player reference for the Blood Hour interactive light novel. Some mysteries and story revelations are intentionally excluded to avoid spoilers.

01 — The World

Blood Hour is a supernatural phenomenon that has existed since 2016. The story takes place on real-world Earth in 2026, with Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia as its primary setting.

19:00:00

Blood Hour begins. Ordinary time freezes and the sky turns blood red.

12 Hours

Awakened experience twelve hours while the ordinary world remains frozen.

19:00:01

The ordinary world resumes. To ordinary humans, only one second has passed.

Who can move?

  • Awakened individuals can move and act.
  • Blood Hour entities can move and act.
  • Ordinary humans become identical coffins.
  • Animals, vehicles, liquids, fire and ordinary environmental activity freeze.

02 — Survival Rules

Human Coffins

Each coffin has an identification plate containing the person's name, age, date of birth, biological sex and nationality.

Permanent Death

Destroying an ordinary person's coffin causes permanent death. The body appears when Blood Hour ends.

Awakened Death

An Awakened who dies becomes a coffin. Their death is permanent. Resurrection is not possible.

Blood Hour is not exclusively a combat environment. Awakened individuals may work, study, explore, trade, train, socialize or rest.

03 — Ability Progression

Every Awakened possesses an ability that can develop through five stages.

01. Awakening

The initial stage of an ability.

02. Emergence

The ability begins to develop further.

03. Ascension

A significant advancement in ability potential.

04. Apex

The final standard progression stage.

05. True Awakening

A special stage requiring specific conditions.

Experience System

  • Experience is tracked from 0/100 to 100/100.
  • At 100/100, the next stage activates automatically and EXP resets.
  • Progression applies through Apex.
  • True Awakening requires Apex, Ability Use 300/300 and one Orb of Awakening.
  • The Orb must be manually used and is consumed permanently.

Ability Use

Every genuine ability activation counts toward the 300-use requirement. Passive effects do not repeatedly count. Failed activations do not count.

04 — The Seven Towers

Seven mysterious towers have appeared at fixed locations around Shah Alam. Tower progression is sequential.

TOWER I

Tower of Ruins

Location: UiTM Shah Alam

7 Floors · Ancient ruins, decay and undead.

TOWER II

Tower of Glass

Location: i-City, Section 7

12 Floors · Glass, reflections, crystals and illusions.

TOWER III

Tower of Verdant

Location: Taman Botani Negara Shah Alam

18 Floors · Mutated plants, roots and parasites.

TOWER IV

Tower of Depths

Location: Taman Tasik Shah Alam, Section 14

25 Floors · Deep sea, aquatic creatures and abyssal ruins.

TOWER V

Tower of Judgement

Location: Dataran Shah Alam

35 Floors · Gothic architecture and infernal judgement.

TOWER VI

Tower of Iron

Location: Kompleks Sukan Shah Alam

50 Floors · Mechanical warfare and industrial fortresses.

TOWER VII

Tower of Silence

Location: Setia City, Setia Alam

70 Floors · Forgotten worlds, endless void and distorted reality.

Tower Progression

  • Floors unlock sequentially after their required objectives are completed.
  • Progression may require exploration, defeating guardians or completing a main challenge.
  • A progression seal must be activated to unlock the next floor.
  • A tower is completed after all required floors are explored and its Final Boss is defeated.
  • Completed towers disappear permanently.

05 — Economy & Equipment

Blood Coin (BC)

The supernatural currency used during Blood Hour.

RM100 = 1 BC

Currency Conversion

RM can be converted into BC. BC cannot be converted back into RM.

Red Exchange

A supernatural marketplace for buying, selling and trading goods and materials.

The Ember Anvil

A blacksmith workshop for weapon repair, crafting and enhancement.

Weapon Quality

Standard → Refined → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythical

Weapon quality improves suitable characteristics. It does not automatically grant unrelated abilities.

Important Equipment Rules

  • Personal weapons are bound to their owners.
  • Weapons materialize during Blood Hour and disappear when it ends.
  • Weapons cannot be carried into the ordinary world.
  • Materials can be sold, traded, crafted or used for repairs and enhancement.

06 — Factions

Different groups have emerged among the Awakened. Their goals, beliefs and methods are not necessarily the same.

UiTM Student Group

A group of students connected to UiTM Shah Alam, navigating survival and the mysteries of Blood Hour.

Truth Seeker

A group seeking answers about Blood Hour, its origins and the truth behind the phenomenon.

Blood Cult

A faction with its own ambitions and beliefs concerning Blood Hour.

Faction membership does not determine an individual's morality, strength or personal motivations.
