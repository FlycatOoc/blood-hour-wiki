THE PHENOMENON

A World Beyond Ordinary Time

Blood Hour is a mysterious phenomenon that occurs every day at exactly 19:00:00. Ordinary time comes to a standstill, the sky turns blood red, and the world enters a twelve-hour period governed by unfamiliar rules.

Only the Awakened and entities associated with Blood Hour remain active. For everyone else, the world is frozen in the moment the phenomenon begins.
