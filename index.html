01 / THE PHENOMENON

A SECOND
THAT LASTS
TWELVE HOURS.

Blood Hour is a daily twelve-hour interval hidden inside a single second of ordinary time.

At exactly 19:00:00, ordinary time freezes. The sky turns blood red, ordinary humans become coffins, and the world falls silent. Only the Awakened and entities of Blood Hour can move.

At 07:00 from the Awakened perspective, ordinary time resumes at 19:00:01. To everyone else, only one second has passed.

01
◷

The Daily Freeze

Blood Hour begins at precisely 19:00:00 every day. Weather, vehicles, fire, liquids and ordinary activity are frozen in place.

02
▣

The Coffins

Every ordinary human becomes an identical coffin bearing their identifying details. Their bodies return when normal time resumes.

03
†

Permanent Death

Destroying an ordinary person's coffin kills them permanently. An Awakened who dies also becomes a coffin. There is no resurrection.
