THE PHENOMENON

What Is Blood Hour?

Blood Hour is a mysterious phenomenon that occurs every day at exactly 19:00:00. Ordinary time comes to a standstill, the sky turns blood red, and the world enters a twelve-hour period governed by unfamiliar rules.

Only the Awakened and entities associated with Blood Hour remain active. For everyone else, the world is frozen in the moment the phenomenon begins.

SURVIVAL KNOWLEDGE

Blood Hour Rules

Understanding these rules is essential for survival.

01

Daily Activation

Blood Hour begins every day at exactly 19:00:00. The phenomenon lasts twelve hours for its participants.

02

Time Suspension

Ordinary time stops during Blood Hour. When normal time resumes at 19:00:01, only one second has passed outside.

03

The Blood-Red Sky

The sky changes to a blood-red colour whenever Blood Hour begins.

04

Frozen Environment

Ordinary humans, animals, vehicles, water, fire, weather, and normal environmental activity remain frozen.

05

Human Coffins

Ordinary humans are transformed into identical coffins. Each coffin carries an identity plate.

06

Permanent Death

Destroying an ordinary human's coffin results in permanent death. The death of an Awakened is also permanent.

07

Awakened Activity

Awakened individuals can move and act during Blood Hour, unlike ordinary humans.

08

No Resurrection

Death during Blood Hour is final. There is no known resurrection.

II

III

IV

Apex

V

True Awakening

The highest recognised ability stage.

Ability stages are separate from monster classification.
