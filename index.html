THE PHENOMENON · 01

A World
Beyond Ordinary Time

Blood Hour is a supernatural phenomenon that exists alongside ordinary reality. It first appeared in 2016. By 2026, it has existed for approximately ten years, remaining unknown to most people living their normal lives.

Every day at exactly 19:00:00, ordinary time stops. The sky turns blood red, ordinary humans transform into identical coffins, and the environment is suspended in place. Only Awakened individuals and Blood Hour entities remain active.

Those who experience it receive twelve additional hours to move, work, investigate, explore, trade, fight or simply live. When the phenomenon ends, ordinary time resumes at exactly 19:00:01. Only one second has passed outside Blood Hour.

FIXED EXCHANGE RATE

Blood Coin Economy

RM100 = 1 BC

Blood Coin (BC) is the official currency used within the Blood Hour economy. It exists as a supernatural digital balance associated with an Awakened individual's identity.

Malaysian Ringgit can be converted into BC, but BC cannot be converted back into Malaysian Ringgit. BC has no direct purchasing value in ordinary life.

BC balances persist between Blood Hour sessions. Transactions, purchases, sales, crafting expenses and material ownership must remain consistent.

The Coffin System

Ordinary humans transform into identical coffins when Blood Hour begins. Each coffin carries an identity plate containing the person's full name, age, date of birth, biological sex and nationality.

Awakened

Awakened individuals remain active during Blood Hour. Their abilities can support combat, defence, mobility, awareness, investigation, healing or other forms of survival.

Permanent Death

An Awakened who dies during Blood Hour remains dead. Destroying an ordinary person's coffin also causes permanent death. Resurrection is impossible through the established system.

Ability Use

Every genuine ability activation counts as one use. Failed activations do not count. Passive ongoing effects do not repeatedly count without a new activation.

True Awakening

True Awakening requires the Apex stage, 300 Ability Uses and one Orb of Awakening. The Orb must be manually activated and is consumed immediately.

Personal Weapons

Established personal weapons materialise during Blood Hour and dematerialise when it ends. They cannot be carried into ordinary time.
